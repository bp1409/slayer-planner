## Source disagreements

- Vampire access requires reaching Rift Maddox at Stillgore Château. The general Slayer wiki entry lists a Tier II Sven completion, while the Riftstalker entry lists Tier III. The planner prompts for access confirmation without assuming a specific Sven requirement. [General requirements](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Slayer#Requirements) · [Boss requirements](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Riftstalker_Bloodfiend#Spawning)
- The same two pages disagree on whether Vampire IV requires Combat 15. The planner displays that uncertainty and does not enforce it as a numeric gate. Both pages list Combat 25 and previous-tier completion for Vampire V, which lacks the Slayer level 7 requirement found on live coin Tier V quests. [General requirements](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Slayer#Requirements) · [Boss requirements](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Riftstalker_Bloodfiend#Spawning)
- The live release links an Alpha 5 Revenant sheet. A later Alpha 6 note changes Tier V Revenant Flesh from 64 to 32–64, which the current wiki confirms. The planner consequently uses the current wiki for the other disputed Zombie figures, with an on-screen note. The earlier sheet's II–IV Flesh amounts are 8–16, 16–32 and 32–48. The wiki lists 4–8, 8–16 and 16–32. The sheet lists 0.5% and 1% Paragon Shard rates on IV and V, while the wiki lists 0.1% and 0.2%, consistent with the release description of reduced rates. Tier IV Scythe Blade is 0.2% on the sheet and 0.1% on the wiki. The II–IV Flesh amounts, Tier IV Scythe Blade rate and exact Paragon rates still need a live check. [Hypixel Alpha 5 sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vR89yuHTiqhko583imFPZSokHYdgZ6N7gWsOXqJnduD3xTii5L9z2lCTzYA1zC80OJkEsRhDPpVahJX/pubhtml) · [Alpha 6 notes](https://hypixel.net/threads/november-10-magic-find-rework-testing-6-accessory-bag-revamp-economy-changes-testing-3.6170932/) · [Wiki drop table](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Revenant_Horror#Drops)
- Vampire II's Fang-tastic Chocolate Chip requires level 3 in the official sheet and level 4 on the wiki. The display uses the sheet's level with an uncertainty note. [Hypixel release sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRsDiYGGkmU33X_ff2HND4bjuiNoh2MNKntkpb0X4FbL5ndC3tBJV-VNg44ExGAmADW6rd0d25EJ2s2/pubhtml) · [Wiki drop table](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Riftstalker_Bloodfiend#Drops)
- The official Dye Changes text updates Sangria rates after the values shown in the embedded Riftstalker sheet. The planner uses the final release text's rates of 1 in 25,000, 20,000, 15,000, 10,000 and 5,000, which also match the current boss page. The Voidgloom sheet calls an item “Exceedingly Rare Ender Artifact Upgrader”. The planner keeps the wiki item name “Exceedingly Rare Ender Artifact Upgrade” with the release sheet's rate, quantity and level. [0.27.3 release](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-27-3-magic-find-rework-accessory-bag-revamp-and-more.6169127/) · [Ender Artifact crafting](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Artifact#Crafting)
